# Trivi — Your Side-Brain for Trivial Tasks

You're reading an article and hit a word you half-know. You're coding and can't remember a bash command. You need a quick translation.

What do you do? Open a new tab, Google it, lose your flow. Or ask your AI chat and pollute the thread with a one-word question.

**Trivi fixes this.** One tap, type, Enter, done. Back to what you were doing.

- **Translate** — type any word, get all your languages at once
- **Explain** — `e what is a reverse proxy` → 3-sentence answer
- **Command** — `c find files larger than 100mb` → just the command, ready to copy
- **Question** — `q capital of Uzbekistan` → Tashkent

No accounts. No subscriptions. No data collection. Your API key stays in your browser — the app has no server.

Add your own custom commands in Settings. 20KB total. Works on any device.
